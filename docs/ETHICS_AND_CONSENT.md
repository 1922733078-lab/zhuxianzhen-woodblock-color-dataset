# Ethics and Informed Consent Compliance

## 1. Ethical Governance and Helsinki Compliance

The human observer visual perception experiments reported in this study strictly complied with the ethical principles for medical research involving human subjects established in the **World Medical Association Declaration of Helsinki** (amended 2013).

- **Nature of Experiment:** Purely non-invasive psychophysical visual observation under controlled standard darkroom conditions.
- **Physical Environment:** Conforming to **ISO 3664:2009** viewing booth standards, utilizing a factory-calibrated D65 standard reference display.
- **No Inherent Risk:** The procedure involved no invasive operations, no pharmaceuticals, no ionizing radiation, and zero risk of physiological or psychological harm.

---

## 2. Participant Cohorts and De-Identification

A total of 20 voluntary human observers participated in the two-alternative forced-choice (2AFC) study:
- **Senior Faculty Expert Cohort ($n=8$):** Designated anonymously as `E01` through `E08`, holding senior academic appointments and professional expertise in traditional Chinese art, woodblock printing, and visual communication design.
- **Trainee Designer Cohort ($n=12$):** Designated anonymously as `N01` through `N12`, comprising senior undergraduate and postgraduate students majoring in design disciplines with formal color theory training.

### Strict Privacy & Anonymization Safeguards:
1. **Zero Personal Identifiers:** All personal identities (including legal names, student/employee IDs, ages, genders, and phone numbers) were scrubbed prior to dataset aggregation.
2. **Irreversible Coding:** Observers are referenced exclusively by their anonymized alphanumeric codes (`E01–E08`, `N01–N12`) across all trial logs, summary tables, and scripts.
3. **Written Informed Consent:** Every participant received comprehensive oral and written briefing regarding the research objectives, task protocols, and voluntary withdrawal rights, signing a standard written informed consent agreement prior to experimental initialization.
